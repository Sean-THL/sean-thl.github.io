Project · Mechanical design

Micro-soft Aerial Robotics

Pneumatic micro-muscle modeling and experimental characterization for bio-inspired micro aerial robots.

Project overview

This collaborative research explores pneumatically actuated, bio-inspired micro aerial robots. The research connects nonlinear actuator models with instrumentation and experimental measurements.

Developed experimental instrumentation and data-acquisition workflows for pneumatically actuated aerial robots, including design, simulation and mathematical modeling. Built image-processing and tracking workflows to measure small actuator displacements from high-speed video and characterize dynamic response across operating conditions.

Developed physics-based and semi-empirical models of nonlinear pneumatic actuator behavior and compared model predictions with experimental measurements.
