Project · Mechanical design

Microscale Bio-Inspired Soft Aerial Robotics

Pneumatic micro-muscle modeling and experimental characterization for bio-inspired micro aerial robots.

Research question

How can nonlinear pneumatic micro-muscles be modeled and measured well enough to support controlled flight in sub-gram aerial robots?

My contribution

  • Developed experimental instrumentation and data-acquisition workflows for pneumatically actuated aerial robots.
  • Built image-processing and tracking methods to measure small actuator displacements from high-speed video.
  • Developed physics-based and semi-empirical actuator models and compared their predictions with experimental measurements.

Results & current status

The instrumentation and modeling supported a collaborative study of controlled hovering flight. I co-authored the related manuscript, with contributions centered on actuator modeling, measurement, and experimental workflows.
