Research · Safe autonomy

SEAMLiS

Perception-aware safety for multi-robot exploration, from simulation to Crazyflie hardware.

Project overview

Limited sensing can leave a robot moving toward an obstacle while its sensor looks elsewhere. The team developed a safety framework for exploration under these perception constraints.

Supported deployment of the system on Crazyflie platforms, integrated planning, control and motion capture through ROS interfaces and coordinate transformations, and conducted hardware experiments.

Work focused on the interface between the team’s planning and safety modules and physical flight. Investigated controller instability, estimation error, communication latency and model mismatch.

Single-robot and multi-agent hardware experiments were conducted in GPS-denied environments. The supplied material does not establish a quantitative performance comparison for the deployment work.
