Project · Mechanical design

Bio-Inspired Manta-Ray Soft Robotics: Design, Modelling, Simulation and Control

Modeling, fabrication and testing of soft pneumatic locomotion.

Project overview

Developed a bio-inspired manta-ray locomotion system using pneumatic actuators. Implemented Piecewise Constant Curvature kinematics in MATLAB for undulation and oscillation control.

Used Abaqus finite-element analysis to investigate actuator behavior under pressures from 30 to 100 kPa. Fabricated and tested soft pneumatic actuators to validate the simulation and develop a feasible robot prototype.
