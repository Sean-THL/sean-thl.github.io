Project · Perception

TEG-SLAM

Temporal evidence and memory-aware pose estimation in a 3D Gaussian SLAM pipeline.

Research question

Can temporal evidence improve camera trajectory estimation and scene reconstruction in a 3D Gaussian SLAM pipeline relative to Wild-GS SLAM?

My contribution

  • Developed a memory-aware pose-estimation module that incorporates information across successive frames.
  • Modified Lie-group state updates and integrated temporal features into the tracking and mapping optimization pipeline.
  • Evaluated trajectory accuracy and reconstruction quality against the Wild-GS SLAM baseline.

Results & current status

Current evaluation against the Wild-GS SLAM baseline reports an 18% reduction in absolute trajectory error and 2–5% improvements in PSNR and SSIM. Ongoing work is documenting the evaluated datasets, sequence-level aggregation, and full protocol for reproducibility.
