About

Tzu-Hsiang (Sean) Lin

I am a graduate student in Mechanical Engineering (Robotics) at the University of Michigan in the USA. I graduate from University of Nottingham in the UK with the first honour class degree in Mechanical Engineering. My interests span safe planning and control, aerial autonomy, perception, and the mechanical design of robotic systems.

At Michigan, research spans multi-agent aerial systems in the Distributed Autonomous System and Control (DASC) Lab, pneumatic micro-actuation in the Zoetic Robotics Lab, and magnetic microrobot control in the SAM Lab. Current work includes a 3D Gaussian Splatting aerial simulator connecting scene reconstruction, visual simulation, and future sim-to-real deployment.

Previously, I work in Advanced Robotics Laboratory at the University of Nottingham working on the Underwater Bio-inspired Soft Robotics. This summer, I worked as a Product Design Engineering Intern at Apple. I have also worked in the aerospace industry for eVTOL development at Bellwether Industries. The long-term goal is to develop complete robotic platforms that bring hardware design and autonomy together.
