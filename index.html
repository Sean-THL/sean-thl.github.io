About

I am a graduate student in Mechanical Engineering (Robotics) at the University of Michigan in the USA. I graduated from the University of Nottingham in the UK with a first-class honours degree in Mechanical Engineering. My interests span safe planning and control, trajectory optimization, SLAM, and multi-robot systems for aerial robotics.

At Michigan, research spans multi-agent aerial systems and safe control in the Distributed Autonomous System and Control (DASC) Lab, pneumatic micro-actuation in the Zoetic Robotics Lab, and magnetic microrobot control in the SAM Lab. Current work includes a 3D Gaussian Splatting aerial simulator connecting scene reconstruction, visual simulation, and future sim-to-real deployment.

Previously, I worked in the Advanced Robotics Laboratory at the University of Nottingham on bio-inspired underwater soft robotics. I also worked as a Product Design Engineering Intern at Apple and contributed to eVTOL development at Bellwether Industries. My long-term goal is to develop complete robotic platforms that bring hardware design and autonomy together.
